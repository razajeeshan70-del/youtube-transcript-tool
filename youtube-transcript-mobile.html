How to Get a YouTube Transcript on Mobile for Free

Need the spoken words from a YouTube video while using your phone? A transcript can help you review a lesson, make notes, find key points, or read along without replaying the entire video.

Get a Transcript with Our Free Online Tool

  1. Open the YouTube app or website and choose a video.
  2. Tap Share and copy the video link.
  3. Open our transcript tool in your mobile browser.
  4. Paste the link and tap Generate Transcript.
  5. If transcript text is available, copy it or download it as a TXT file.

Can You Get a Transcript Directly on YouTube?

Some YouTube videos offer a built-in transcript. Open the video, expand its description or video details, and look for the transcript option if available. The exact location can vary by app version and video.

Why Might a Transcript Not Be Available?

Not every video has accessible transcript text. Captions may be unavailable, the transcript service may not support the video, or a temporary service limit may prevent a result. Try another video or check whether YouTube provides captions for it.

Can I Copy or Save the Transcript?

When the tool successfully returns transcript text, use Copy Transcript to copy it or Download TXT to save a text file. Review important names, numbers, and technical terms against the original video because automatic captions can contain errors.

Is the Mobile Transcript Tool Free?

The tool is available online without installing a separate app. Transcript availability depends on the video and the connected transcript service; a result cannot be guaranteed for every video.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install an app?

No separate app is needed to open the tool in your phone's browser.

Can I download a transcript on my phone?

Yes, when a transcript is generated successfully, you can use the Download TXT option.

Will every YouTube video work?

No. Some videos may not have accessible transcript text or may not be supported by the service.
