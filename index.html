How to Get a YouTube Transcript

A YouTube transcript displays spoken words from a video as text. It can help you review information, find important points, and make notes without replaying the entire video.

Step 1: Copy the video link

Open the YouTube video you want to use and copy its URL. Make sure you have copied the actual video link rather than a channel link.

Step 2: Generate the transcript

Paste the URL into the input box above and select Generate Transcript. The tool sends the video URL to its server-side transcript service to request available transcript text.

Step 3: Copy or save the text

If a transcript is returned successfully, review the text and use Copy Transcript or Download TXT to keep a copy for your notes. Check names, numbers, and technical terms against the original video when accuracy matters.

Common Transcript Problems and Solutions

No transcript is available

Some videos may not have an accessible transcript, or the transcript service may not support them. Try another video and check whether captions or transcript text are available.

The video URL is invalid

Make sure the link points to an individual YouTube video. Copy the URL again and check that it contains a valid video ID.

The tool shows an error or request limit

Temporary service issues, API limits, or network problems can prevent a request from completing. Wait before trying again. If the issue continues, use the Contact Us page to report it.

What Can You Use a Transcript For?

  • Study and revision: Review lessons, lectures, and explanations in text form.
  • Research and note-taking: Find useful points from interviews, talks, and educational videos.
  • Content planning: Organize ideas from videos you are permitted to use.
  • Accessibility: Read available spoken content when reading is more convenient than listening.

Important Limitations

This tool does not guarantee a transcript for every video. Results depend on transcript availability and the capabilities of the configured service. Automatically generated text may contain mistakes, missing words, or incorrect punctuation.

Use Transcripts Responsibly

Respect copyright and the rights of video creators. Use transcript text in accordance with applicable laws and the relevant platform terms. Do not republish someone else's content without the necessary permission.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a YouTube transcript?

A transcript is the written text of the spoken words in a video.

Can every video be transcribed?

Not always. Availability depends on the video, the transcript service and its support for that video.

Is my API key visible on this page?

The frontend does not contain the API key. Keep SUPADATA_API_KEY configured in Vercel Environment Variables and use it only on the server.

Can I download the transcript?

Yes. When a transcript is generated successfully, use Copy Transcript or Download TXT.
