What is Transcript Tools?

Transcript Tools is a simple online utility designed to help users extract transcripts from supported YouTube videos. You can paste a YouTube video URL, generate the available transcript, and then copy or download the resulting text.

We aim to keep the experience straightforward, easy to understand, and convenient to use on both mobile phones and desktop devices.

A note about availability

Transcript availability can depend on the YouTube video and the transcript service. Some videos may not return a transcript when suitable captions or supported transcript data are unavailable.
