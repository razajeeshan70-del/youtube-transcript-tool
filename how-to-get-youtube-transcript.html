How to Get a YouTube Transcript Online for Free

A YouTube transcript displays the spoken words from a video as text. It can help you review lessons, find important information, take notes, or read video content instead of listening to the entire recording.

How to Get a YouTube Transcript

  1. Open the YouTube video you want to transcribe.
  2. Copy the video's URL from the share option or browser address bar.
  3. Open our free YouTube transcript tool.
  4. Paste the video URL into the input field.
  5. Click the Generate Transcript button and wait for the result.
  6. If a transcript is available, copy the text or download it as a TXT file.

Can Every YouTube Video Be Transcribed?

No. Transcript availability depends on the video's captions, the transcript service, and whether the video is supported. Some videos may not have an available transcript, and some requests may fail.

Why Use a YouTube Transcript?

  • Review important points from educational videos.
  • Make searchable notes for study or research.
  • Quote or reference spoken information more easily.
  • Save available transcript text for later reading.

Is the Transcript Always Accurate?

Not necessarily. Automatic captions and transcripts may contain spelling mistakes, incorrect names, or punctuation errors. Review important information against the original video.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this YouTube transcript tool free?

You can try the tool online for free. Successful results depend on video support and the transcript service's availability.

Can I download a transcript?

Yes. When a transcript is generated successfully, use the Download TXT option if it is available.

Why is a transcript unavailable?

The video may not have an accessible transcript, or the service may not support that video. Try another supported video URL.
