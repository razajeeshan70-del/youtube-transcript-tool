How to Get a YouTube Transcript Online for Free

A YouTube transcript turns the spoken words in a video into text. It can help you study lessons, review tutorials, research a topic, find important information, or take notes without replaying the entire video. You can use YouTube's own transcript feature when available or try an online YouTube transcript tool.

Method 1: Use YouTube's Built-In Transcript Feature

  1. Open the YouTube video you want to read.
  2. Expand the video description or its details.
  3. Look for the "Show transcript" option, if available.
  4. Open the transcript and review the displayed text.
  5. Copy the text if the interface and available options allow it.

YouTube's interface can vary between desktop and mobile devices. The transcript option may not appear for every video.

Method 2: Use an Online YouTube Transcript Tool

If you want to generate a transcript through an online tool, follow these steps:

  1. Open the YouTube video and copy its URL.
  2. Visit our YouTube transcript tool.
  3. Paste the video link into the input field.
  4. Click "Generate Transcript" and wait for the result.
  5. If successful, copy the transcript or download it as a TXT file.

Why Use a YouTube Transcript?

  • Study: Review lessons and tutorials at your own pace.
  • Research: Search text for important points and terms.
  • Note-taking: Save useful information for later.
  • Accessibility: Read spoken content when listening is inconvenient.
  • Content review: Find sections you want to revisit in the video.

Why Is a YouTube Transcript Unavailable?

A transcript may be unavailable if captions are missing or inaccessible, the video is unsupported, or the transcript service cannot retrieve its text. Temporary service errors or request limits can also affect results.

Try another supported video URL. If the video has a transcript on YouTube, you can also try its built-in "Show transcript" option.

Are YouTube Transcripts Always Accurate?

No. Automatically generated captions can contain mistakes in spelling, punctuation, names, and technical terms. Check important information against the original video before using or quoting it.
