Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Transcript Tools. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our YouTube transcript extraction service.

We aim to keep the service simple and respect your privacy.

2. Information You Provide

When you use the transcript tool, you may enter or submit a YouTube video URL in order to request a transcript.

We do not ask you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number, or address to use the basic transcript tool.

3. How Transcript Requests Work

When you submit a supported YouTube URL, the request is processed through our server-side transcript service so that the available transcript can be returned to you.

The service may use third-party technology providers to process transcript requests and operate the website.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Transcript Tools does not require cookies for the basic transcript-generation functionality.

Third-party services used by the website may use their own technologies, cookies, or similar mechanisms according to their own policies.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may rely on third-party services to provide infrastructure, hosting, or transcript-processing functionality. These providers may process technical information necessary to provide their services.

Third-party services are governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the website and its server-side configuration. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Transcript Tools is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes to the website, services, or applicable requirements. The updated version will be published on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the way the website handles information, please contact us through the Contact Us page.
